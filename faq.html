Frequently Asked Questions

What is Birding?

It is another term for birdwatching or the activity of observing birds in their natural habitat.

What do I need to Start birding?

  • Binoculars
  • Bottle of Water
  • Snacks
  • Field Guide
  • Camera
  • Notepad

You don't need all the items in this list; most birders only use buinoculars and a field guide. You can enjoy birding with just your enthusiasm and curiosity!

Is there a certain time of year to bird?

Birding can be enjoyed year-round, but some seasons may offer unique opportunities to see certain species or behaviors.

  1. Spring: This is prime migration and breeding season. Birds display colorful plumage and songbirds are singing their tunes.
  2. Summer: Young birds may be seen learning to fly and forage. This is a good time to see the difference between juvenile and adult plumage.
  3. Fall: Surprisingly, bird activity is at an all-time high during this time. Large flocks of birds migrate south for the winter which isimpressive to witness.
  4. Winter: This can be a challenging time for birding, but local birds are still present. Hardy birds that have adapted to cold weather, such as chickadees and woodpeckers will still be present.

Do I need to be an expert on birds to join?

Not at all! Whether you’re a fledgling at birding or a seasoned vet with eagle eyes, people from any level can join us!

Do I need to travel far to bird?

No, you can enjoy birding in your own backyard or local parks! Check this page out if you would like to make a garden that attracts birds: Making a Bird-Friendly Garden in Chicago

Do I need to pay to join the group?

No, joining the Chicago Avian Adventure Collective is completely free! All you need to do is show up and enjoy the company of fellow birders. If you would like to contribute to our efforts, we welcome any donations or volunteer work!
