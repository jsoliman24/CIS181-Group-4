Rules of the CAAC

We here at CAAC take birding seriously so there are rules to follow when you particitpate in our activities:

  1. Respect the birds and their habitats
  2. Follow all local laws and regulations
  3. Do not disturb nesting sites or breeding areas
  4. Keep noise to a minimum to avoid startling birds
  5. Do not feed birds in the wild
  6. Respect other birders
