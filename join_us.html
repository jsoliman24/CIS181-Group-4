Why Join?

Chicago is one of the best cities for bird watching. By joining us you can meet many other bird enthusiasts, from casuals to veterans and enjoy the birds the windy city has to offer. Everyone is welcome! It's free to join and a great way to get outside and meet people.

Member Benefits

  • Guided bird walks at parks and along the lakefront
  • Monthly newsletter with future events
  • Discounts at local birding gear stores
  • Free beginner workshops and ID tip sheets
  • A friendly community of people
